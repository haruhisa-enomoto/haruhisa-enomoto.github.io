## Comment

For a finite-dimensional algebra over any field, E-finiteness is equivalent to the existence of nonzero maps in both directions between any two nonzero finite-dimensional modules with the same dimension vector. The paper gives several other characterizations, making a number of conjectures on brick-finiteness equivalent. It also proves that every E-finite algebra with at most two isomorphism classes of simple modules is brick-finite.
