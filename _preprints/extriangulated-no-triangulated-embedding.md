## Comment

The example cannot be embedded fully faithfully as an extriangulated category into any triangulated category. In contrast, any countable family of its objects is contained in a full extension-closed subcategory admitting an embedding into an algebraic triangulated category. Version 2 adds a formalization linked from the Hexagon record.
