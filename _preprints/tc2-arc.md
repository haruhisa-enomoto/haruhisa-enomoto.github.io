## Comment

For Artin algebras over a fixed commutative artinian ring, we establish global equivalence of the Auslander-Reiten conjecture, the generalised Nakayama conjecture, the Nakayama conjecture, and both Tachikawa conjectures.

For finite-dimensional algebras over a fixed field, we also show that derived invariance of the Nakayama conjecture is equivalent to its validity for all such algebras, answering a question of Chen and Xi.
