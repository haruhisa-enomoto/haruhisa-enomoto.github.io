## Comment

The intersections of the two halves of pretorsion theories are precisely the image-closed subcategories, and the pretorsion theories form a complete lattice. For a linearly oriented quiver of type A, the two families are counted by Genocchi and median Genocchi numbers, respectively, with Gandhi polynomial refinements.
