## Comment

The periodicity conjecture asks whether a finite-dimensional algebra is periodic whenever all its simple modules are periodic. We construct a 36-dimensional counterexample whose simple modules have period four. The algebra has a 2-dimensional nonperiodic module, which shows that the algebra itself is not periodic.
